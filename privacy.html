Privacy Policy

NMF Stats - last updated November 16, 2026

NMF Stats is a personal analytics tool used only by the owner of the Nice Math Facts TikTok account.

Data collected

With the account owner's authorization, the app reads basic profile information (display name, avatar, follower count) and public statistics of the owner's own videos (title, publish time, views, likes, comments, shares).

How data is used

The data is stored locally on the owner's own computer and is used only to display a private analytics dashboard. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other users

The app does not collect data about any other TikTok users.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner uses the tool. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings under Security > Manage app permissions, and the local data can be deleted at any time.

Contact

Questions can be sent via direct message to the Nice Math Facts TikTok account.
